Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

513Sips uses the information you share to respond to inquiries, plan events and improve this website. This page explains what we collect, why we use it and the choices available to you.

Information you choose to share

When you submit an inquiry, email us or call us, we may receive details such as your name, contact information, event date, venue, guest count and the plans you include in your message. We use that information to respond, prepare recommendations and provide the services you request.

Website analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors find the site, which pages they view and which actions lead to an inquiry. Google Analytics may use first-party cookies and similar identifiers. We do not send names, email addresses, phone numbers or form contents to Google Analytics.

We also use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors move through the site through heatmaps, interaction data and session recordings. Clarity may collect clicks, scrolling, page activity, device information and diagnostic data. Sensitive form fields are masked by default, and we do not intentionally include inquiry form contents in recordings.

Service providers

We use service providers to operate the website and receive inquiries. These include Google Analytics, Microsoft Clarity, Formspree and our website hosting provider. They process information for us under their own terms and privacy practices.

Your choices

You can limit cookies through your browser settings. Google also provides a Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking analytics cookies may reduce the information we receive about your visit, but it will not stop you from viewing the site or contacting us.

How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and event information only as long as reasonably needed to respond, provide services, maintain business records and meet legal obligations. Analytics providers retain data according to the settings and policies for their services.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only with service providers that help us run the website and business, when you direct us to or when required by law.

Contact us

To ask a privacy question or request access, correction or deletion of information you shared with us, email contact@513sips.com.
